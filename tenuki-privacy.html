Tenuki Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Tenuki is made by Emilia Walker, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: tenuki[AT]lazerwalker.com.

Summary

Tenuki has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no cookies. Most of your data stays on your device. Tenuki sends data to other services only to report crashes, to send push notifications, to check for app updates, and to connect to Go servers that you choose to log in to.

On your device

Your game history, settings, and board analysis stay on your device. Photos from the camera board scanner are processed on your device and are not uploaded, and AI game analysis happens on your device.

When you log in to a Go server, Tenuki keeps your login details in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore), so that it can log you in again. For Fox, Tenuki keeps a hash of your password, not the password itself. Deleting the app removes this data.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or has an error, it sends a report to Sentry. A report contains technical details about the error, your device model, operating system version, device language and region, app version, and a random identifier for this installation of the app. Reports do not contain your IP address, your messages, or your account names.

We use this data to find and fix bugs, which is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. Sentry keeps reports for up to 90 days. Sentry is in the United States. For more information, see Sentry's mobile privacy information.

Push notifications

If you log in to online-go.com (OGS) and allow notifications, our push server (hosted by Hetzner in Nuremberg, Germany) stores your OGS user ID, an encrypted OGS login token, a device identifier, a push token from Apple or Google, and your notification settings. The server uses these to watch your OGS games and to tell you when it is your turn, or when someone sends you a message.

Notifications go to your device through Apple Push Notification service or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. A notification can contain your opponent's username and the text of a chat message.

We process this data to give you the feature you asked for. When you log out of OGS in Tenuki, we delete it at once. If you stop using the app, we delete it 90 days after you last opened it. Server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for 14 days.

App updates and downloads

The app checks our update server for new versions and downloads files from our download server. Both are hosted by Cloudflare. They do not identify you, and we do not keep logs of these requests.

Go servers you choose to use

If you log in to a Go server, Tenuki sends your login details to that server, and sends your moves and chat messages so that you can play. Each service is responsible for the data it receives. See its privacy policy for details.

  • OGS (online-go.com): You log in on the OGS website in a web browser Tenuki cannot see. The app stores and transmits OAuth tokens only. OGS privacy policy.
  • Pandanet / IGS: Pandanet's IGS connection does not use encryption. This is a limit of Pandanet's service, not of Tenuki: your username, password, moves, and chat messages are sent to Pandanet without encryption. Pandanet privacy policy.
  • Fox: operated by Beijing YeHu Century Technology Co., Ltd. Tenuki sends your username and a hash of your password. You can find Fox's privacy policy in the Fox app.
  • GoProblems (goproblems.com): Tenuki sends your GoProblems API key and your puzzle activity. GoProblems does not publish a privacy policy; contact them through goproblems.com with questions about their data.

International transfers

Sentry, Apple, Google, and Cloudflare can process data in the United States. Where data goes from the EU to these providers, it is protected under the providers' standard EU safeguards. If you use Pandanet or Fox, your data goes to Japan or China, under those services' own terms.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to how we use it. You can do most of this yourself: log out of OGS in Tenuki to delete your push data, or delete the app to remove the data on your device. For anything else, email tenuki[AT]lazerwalker.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Tenuki is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
